Small questions. Simple answers.

A few things to help you find your rhythm.

How do I start a habit?

Choose habits during setup, or open the Habits tab to browse suggestions and create your own. Set a daily or weekly goal and add an optional reminder. Your active habits appear in Today.

How do check-ins and weekly goals work?

Tap a habit in Today to record a check-in. You can undo it if you tapped by mistake. Weekly goals count the days you check in during the week, starting Monday. A quit counter uses your quit date instead of daily check-ins.

Can I fix a missed or accidental check-in?

Yes. Open Progress and tap a day, or choose Edit a day, to adjust that day’s check-ins. You can correct past days, including days before you added the habit. Future days cannot be checked off.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Open a habit and check that Remind me is on and the time is correct. In Settings, check notification permission or use Open iOS notification settings. Your iPhone’s Focus and Scheduled Summary settings can affect when notifications appear. Reminders repeat daily at your chosen local time, including for weekly goals.

How do quit counters work?

Choose a quit habit and set its quit date and time. The counter updates automatically from that date. A future quit date shows a countdown until it starts. You can edit the date or use the counter’s reset option if you want a fresh start.

Can I pause or delete a habit?

In Habits, swipe left to pause or delete. Pausing keeps your history and stops that habit’s reminders; it does not freeze streaks. Deleting a habit also deletes its check-in history from the app.

Where is my data? Can I move it to another phone?

Your habits and progress are stored locally on your iPhone. There is no GoodHabitsBro account, cloud sync or export in this version. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your settings. Read the privacy policy for details.

How do I start over?

In Settings, choose Reset all habits, then confirm Reset all habits and progress. This permanently removes the app’s habits, check-ins, quit counters and reminders from this device and takes you back to habit selection.

Your habits are personal.

No account. No ads. No analytics added to the app. Your progress is yours to keep.
