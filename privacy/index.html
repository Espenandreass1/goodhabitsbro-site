Good Habits Bro is a local habit tracker. You can use the app without creating an account or sending us your habit history.

Privacy policy · Last updated 23 October 2026

1. Who we are

Good Habits Bro is developed by Espen Andreassen. This policy covers the Good Habits Bro iPhone app, this support website and email support. For privacy questions, contact espenandreass1@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores

The app stores information locally on your iPhone so it can show your habits and progress:

  • Habits you select or create, including their names, goals, frequency, icons and colors.
  • Check-in dates, completion history and quit dates used to calculate progress and counters.
  • Reminder times and choices, appearance preferences and setup status.

We do not collect or receive this habit data on a developer-operated server. The app has no account system, advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs. It does not access Apple Health.

3. Optional reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your iPhone using Apple’s notification system. Reminders do not use a Good Habits Bro server or remote push service. You can change reminder settings in the app and revoke notification permission in iOS Settings.

4. Device backups and syncing

Good Habits Bro does not provide its own cloud sync or data export. Depending on your Apple device and account settings, app data may be included in iCloud or computer backups. These backups are managed by Apple or by you; we cannot access them. See Apple’s information about iCloud Backup for more details and controls.

5. Your choices and deletion

Local habit data stays in the app until you remove it. You can edit habits and past check-ins, delete an individual habit and its check-ins, or use Settings → Reset all habits to remove all habits, check-ins, quit counters and reminders from this device.

Deleting the app removes its local data from the iPhone. Earlier device backups may still contain app data; manage those separately in your Apple or computer backup settings. We do not hold a remote copy of your habit history to retrieve or delete.

6. When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request and resolve problems. Please avoid including sensitive habit details unless you want to share them.

Support correspondence is handled through our email provider, Google, and retained only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to ask for access to or deletion of support correspondence. Read Google’s privacy policy for its handling of email services.

7. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have added no analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, contact forms or third-party scripts. Fonts and images are served with the site.

GitHub processes visitor information, including IP addresses, for security and to provide its hosting service. That website processing is separate from the app’s local habit storage. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

8. Sharing and changes

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We do not receive your local habit history. Information you voluntarily send through support may be disclosed if required by applicable law.

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. New features that need permission will ask for it where required.

9. Get in touch

Questions about this policy or your support correspondence? Email espenandreass1@gmail.com. For help using the app, visit Good Habits Bro Support.
